undertow

A music visualizer editor in the browser. Load a song, stack visuals behind the artwork, put timed lyrics on top and render an MP4.

Layers include Milkdrop presets, spectrum, waveform, particles, text and looping video. One project holds the landscape, vertical and square cuts. The editor needs no account.
